Provider Billing legal payer convergence
Date: 2026-08-10

Production convergence scope:
- Avantiqo remains the billing operator and customer-facing prepaid wallet owner.
- The legal payer organization is selected separately per external provider.
- Provider payer legal entity and supplier master must belong to the selected payer organization.
- Exactly one ACTIVE payer configuration is allowed per provider; suspended historical records remain auditable.
- Paid provider execution remains blocked until supplier governance and customer prepaid reservation are both ready.
- Finance vendor invoice/AP and supplier invoice-to-usage reconciliation remain canonical.
- Google Ads Payments configuration remains an additional managed-media control and does not imply advertiser BillingSetup approval or authorize spend.

No campaign activation or provider spend was initiated by this convergence.
