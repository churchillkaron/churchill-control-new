Avantiqo Provider Billing scope convergence
Date: 2026-08-11

Canonical commercial model:
- Avantiqo is the platform/operator.
- Organization is the active business scope.
- Entity is the legal/accounting payer inside the organization.
- Supplier Party is the provider counterparty inside the organization.
- No tenant model or tenant terminology is used.

Administration Provider Billing now requires organization_id for reads and writes.
Provider payer mappings cannot cross the active organization scope.
Supplier governance snapshots may be scoped to one payer organization.
The UI derives organization_id from organization runtime and exposes only that organization's entities and supplier parties.
Commercial verification remains mandatory before provider readiness.
Customer funding remains ACTIVE PREPAID Avantiqo wallet only.
No direct customer/provider billing or customer provider payment method is permitted.
No supplier identity, payer evidence, ad activation, provider spend, or media spend is fabricated or triggered by this change.
