Avantiqo provider commercial payer verification convergence
Date: 2026-08-10

- Avantiqo Platform remains the billing operator, not an assumed legal payer company.
- Provider payer organization, legal entity, and supplier master are proposed mappings until commercially verified.
- Verification requires a non-secret supplier billing evidence reference and one of the controlled verification methods.
- Unverified provider supplier accounts remain BLOCKED and cannot become provider-execution ready.
- Exactly one ACTIVE payer account per provider is enforced by database index.
- Existing prepaid wallet reservation and Service execution controls remain mandatory.
- Google Ads Payments selection remains separate from legal payer verification and does not authorize media spend.
- No provider spend or advertising activation was triggered by this convergence.
