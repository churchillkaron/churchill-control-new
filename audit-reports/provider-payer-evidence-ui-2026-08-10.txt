Provider legal payer evidence control deployment
Date: 2026-08-10

Scope:
- Administration -> Integrations -> Provider Billing
- Adds operator-facing commercial payer verification controls.
- Saved payer/supplier mappings remain blocked until evidence is verified.
- Verification evidence types are supplier invoice, provider statement, billing profile, or billing portal reference.
- Only non-secret references are stored in the verification field.
- Customer billing remains Avantiqo prepaid wallet only.
- No customer-owned provider payment method is permitted.
- No provider spend or advertising activation is authorized by this deployment.
