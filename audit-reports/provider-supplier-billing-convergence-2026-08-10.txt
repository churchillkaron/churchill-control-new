AVANTIQO PROVIDER SUPPLIER BILLING CONVERGENCE
Date: 2026-08-10

Canonical commercial invariant
- Every external provider is an Avantiqo supplier.
- Provider invoices or charges Avantiqo, never the customer.
- Customer provider payment methods and direct supplier billing are prohibited.
- Customer usage is funded only through an ACTIVE PREPAID Avantiqo wallet.
- Full customer charge is reserved before paid provider execution.
- Paid provider execution also requires an ACTIVE Avantiqo provider supplier billing account with payer legal entity and supplier party.

Canonical accounting flow
Customer prepaid wallet -> reservation -> provider execution -> Service usage settlement -> customer wallet charge -> provider invoice/charge to Avantiqo -> Finance vendor invoice/AP -> supplier invoice to Service usage reconciliation -> Finance approval/payment.

Architecture
- Existing Service Pricing, Usage, Wallet, Billing and Finance remain canonical.
- Existing parties/supplier_profiles remain supplier master.
- Existing vendor_invoices/accounts_payable remain Finance AP.
- provider_supplier_billing_accounts is configuration/governance only.
- provider_supplier_invoice_allocations is reconciliation only.
- No duplicate billing or payable ledger was introduced.

Safety
- Internal explicit zero-cost administration controls may discover provider billing configuration without a customer paid reservation.
- Paid provider execution cannot use that exception.
- Supplier invoice reconciliation never authorizes payment.
- Finance approval/payment remains required.
- No provider spend or advertising spend was triggered by this convergence.
