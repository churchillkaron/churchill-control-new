# Domain Ownership

## Platform
Owns:
- Runtime
- Registry
- Identity
- Permissions
- Workflow engine
- Document engine
- Channels
- Providers
- AI infrastructure

Does not own:
- Business logic

---

## Commercial
Owns:
- Customers
- Sales
- CRM
- Marketing
- Revenue lifecycle

---

## People
Owns:
- HR
- Staff
- Payroll
- Workforce

---

## Restaurant
Owns:
- Orders
- Sessions
- Menu
- Payments
- Dining experience

---

## Operations
Owns:
- POS execution
- Kitchen execution
- Expo
- Floor execution
- Tasks
- Activities
- Incidents

---

## Supply Chain
Owns:
- Inventory
- Warehouse
- Procurement
- Production
- Recipes
- Batches
- Consumption
- Waste

---

## Finance
Owns:
- Accounting
- GL
- AR
- AP
- Treasury
- Tax
- Reporting

---

## Projects
Owns:
- Projects
- Project execution

---

## Industry Domains

Hotel:
- Rooms
- Guests
- Bookings

Healthcare:
- Patients
- Admissions
- Clinical workflows

Construction:
- Sites
- Projects
- Field execution

---

## Rules

One owner per capability.

No duplicate business domains.

Platform contains no business logic.

Execution domains do not own business documents.
