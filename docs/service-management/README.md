# Service Management

Service Management owns recurring customer service commitments and versioned execution protocols.

Canonical boundary:

- Service Management: service plans, recurrence, contract windows, customer commitments, execution templates.
- Operations: generated work orders, appointment windows, dispatch, assignments, queues, completion evidence.
- Commercial/CRM: customer and party master data.
- People: workforce identity and eligibility.
- Supply Chain/Inventory: materials and consumables.
- Finance: billing and accounting consequences.

The first reference vertical is recurring field service, validated with Pest Control while remaining multi-industry and organization-scoped.
