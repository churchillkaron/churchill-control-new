# CREATIVE DOMAIN

Creative is Avantiqo's organization-driven, industry-neutral creative operating domain.

It converts a business objective and verified organization context into an accountable creative decision, a governed production graph, finished deliverables, release evidence, and learning.

## Consumers

- Marketing
- Sales
- Commercial
- Website Builder
- App Builder
- Webshop Builder
- Documents
- Customer and staff channels
- AI agents
- Any registered Avantiqo domain that requires creative work

- Missions
- Organization and brand context
- Research

- Production planning

## Canonical flow

Business outcome
→ verified organization, brand, audience, asset, market and channel context
→ research
→ one accountable Creative Master Plan
→ registered workflow and capability resolution
→ specialist production runtimes
→ contextual quality and repair
→ governed human approval and release
→ verified outcome learning

## Rules

- Creative is not owned by any industry, company, venue type, deliverable type, provider, or channel.
- Organization and mission evidence define the business problem. Industry labels may inform research but may not select canned creative behavior.
- The Creative Director chooses the medium and deliverable system when the mission does not explicitly constrain them.
- Workflow families and aliases are registry-owned. Runtime code may not maintain parallel media-routing taxonomies.
- The Creative Master Plan is the single creative and production-intent authority.
- Universal workflows must contain explicit Director-authored production steps and quality gates. Downstream planners may not invent default recipes.
- Temporal work remains a specialist runtime behind the same Creative Director contract; it is never the implicit default.
- No silent defaults for channel, audience, style, language, duration, aspect ratio, resolution, frame rate, provider, currency, or production technique.
- Provider prompts are transport-only. They may be serialized from approved structured specifications at the final governed execution boundary, but they are never source of truth and are never persisted as creative direction.
- Provider selection, pricing, wallet, usage, billing, credentials, and execution remain behind Avantiqo Service Runtime governance.
- Exact identity, product, logo, venue, document and brand assets must remain source-faithful when required by evidence, rights, consent, or approved direction.
- Quality evaluation is contextual to the organization, objective, audience, medium and idea while preserving universal standards of craft, originality, truth, brand fidelity, technical integrity and release fitness.
- Weak work must be rejected, repaired or regenerated before the customer sees it. Release remains fail-closed.
- Learning is organization-scoped and evidence-based; it may not convert category stereotypes into creative defaults.
